Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Acme Technologies provides a digital online service. No physical product is sold, so nothing is shipped to an address.

1. How the service is delivered

  • Pro access is delivered electronically and instantly to your account after your payment is confirmed.
  • In most cases Pro is active within a few seconds of payment. If you pay in a UPI app and return to our page, activation happens automatically.
  • In rare cases, when a bank confirms the payment late, activation can take up to 30 minutes. You do not need to pay again.

2. Where to find your Pro status

Log in and open the pricing or account page. It shows that Pro is active and the date it ends.

3. If Pro does not activate

  1. Wait up to 30 minutes and refresh the page.
  2. If money was deducted and Pro is still not active, email support@yourdomain.com with your payment ID or UPI transaction reference. We will activate your plan manually or refund you, as described in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

4. Shipping charges

There are no shipping charges, and no delivery address is required.
