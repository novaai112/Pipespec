      Frequently asked questions

      Will I be charged automatically every month?

      No. Each payment buys one month or one year of Pro. We do not auto-debit your account, so there are no surprise charges. When your plan ends you can renew with one tap.

      Which payment methods are supported?

      In India: all UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM and others), debit and credit cards, netbanking and popular wallets. Outside India: international credit and debit cards.

      How does UPI work on my phone?

      Tap “Pay with UPI” and choose your UPI app. It opens directly, you approve the payment with your UPI PIN, and you return here with Pro already active. On a computer you can scan a QR code with any UPI app instead.

      Can I get a refund?

      Yes, within the refund window described in our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

      Why is the price different in rupees?

      We show a fixed rupee price for customers in India so the amount you see is exactly what you pay. You can switch between ₹ and $ at any time.
