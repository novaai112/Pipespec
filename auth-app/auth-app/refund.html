Refund & Cancellation

Last updated: October 31, 2026

We want you to be happy with Pro. This policy explains how cancellation and refunds work for Acme Technologies.

1. Cancellation

  • Pro is prepaid and does not renew automatically. We never charge you again unless you choose to pay. So there is nothing to cancel and no hidden recurring charge.
  • When your paid period ends, your account returns to the Free plan. Your account and data stay available under Free-plan limits.
  • You can stop using Pro at any time. Access continues until the end of the period you paid for.

2. Refunds

  • 7-day refund: if you are not satisfied with your first Pro purchase (monthly or yearly), email us within 7 days of payment and we will refund the full amount.
  • Later purchases: renewals and repeat purchases are non-refundable once Pro has been active, except in the cases in section 3.
  • Refunds are not available for periods that have already been used after the refund window.

3. Cases that are always refunded

  • Duplicate payment for the same plan made by mistake.
  • Payment debited but Pro not activated and we are unable to fix it.
  • Service not delivered because of a fault on our side.

If your bank or UPI app debited money but the payment shows as failed, the amount is normally reversed automatically by your bank or Razorpay within 5–7 working days. If it is not, email us with your payment ID or UPI transaction reference and we will help.

4. How to request a refund

  1. Email support@yourdomain.com from your registered email address.
  2. Include your payment ID or UPI/bank transaction reference, the date and amount.
  3. We reply within 2 business days and tell you whether the refund is approved.

5. Refund timeline and method

  • Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method (the same UPI account, card, bank or wallet).
  • After we approve, refunds usually reach you in 5–7 business days, depending on your bank. Card refunds can take up to 10 business days.
  • For payments in US Dollars, the amount in your own currency may differ slightly because of exchange rates applied by your bank. We refund the full amount charged to us in the original currency.
  • A full refund ends Pro access for that payment.
