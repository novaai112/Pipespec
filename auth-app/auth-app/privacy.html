Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Acme Technologies (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with applicable law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India).

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and sign-in method. If you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google.
  • Password: stored only as a secure one-way hash. We cannot see or recover your actual password.
  • Payment records: the plan, amount, currency, date, status, payment method type (for example UPI or card) and the Razorpay order and payment IDs. We never receive or store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN or bank credentials.
  • Technical data: basic device and browser information and your approximate time zone, used to show the right currency and keep the Service secure.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and let you log in.
  • To process payments, activate and manage your Pro plan, and prevent fraud.
  • To send essential messages, such as email confirmation, password reset links and payment-related notices.
  • To respond to support requests and to improve the Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations such as tax and accounting rules.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use browser storage only to keep you signed in and to remember choices such as your preferred currency. We do not use advertising cookies. Razorpay may set its own cookies while you pay.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the Service:

  • Supabase – database, authentication and hosting of our backend.
  • Razorpay – payment processing (governed by Razorpay’s own privacy policy).
  • Google – only if you choose “Continue with Google”.
  • Authorities, when we are required to do so by law.

5. Data retention

We keep your account data while your account is active. Payment records are kept for as long as the law requires for tax and accounting purposes. When you delete your account we remove your profile and personal details, except records we must keep by law.

6. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and database rules that let each user read only their own data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, download or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by emailing support@yourdomain.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and in any case within the period required by law.

8. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 18. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date shows the latest version.

10. Grievance and contact

For privacy questions or complaints, contact: Acme Technologies, Your full postal address, City, State, PIN, India. Email: support@yourdomain.com. Phone: +91 00000 00000. Hours: Mon–Sat, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM IST.
